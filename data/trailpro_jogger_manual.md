# TrailPro Jogger Stroller User Manual

**Model:** TrailPro TJ-200  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Manual Type:** Synthetic portfolio manual for RAG evaluation project

---

## 1. Product Overview

The TrailPro Jogger Stroller is a single-child jogging stroller designed for walking, jogging, and active outdoor use on approved surfaces.

The TrailPro Jogger may be used for jogging only when all jogging safety requirements are met. It is not intended for skating, biking, stair use, escalator use, or rough off-road terrain.

---

## 2. Child Weight and Age Limits

The TrailPro Jogger Stroller may be used only when the child meets all of the following requirements:

- The child can sit upright without assistance.
- The child weighs no more than **55 lb**.
- The child is secured using the built-in five-point harness.
- The child fits fully inside the seat without leaning outside the stroller frame.

Do not use the stroller if the child weighs more than **55 lb**.

Do not place more than one child in the TrailPro Jogger Stroller.

---

## 3. Jogging Age Requirement

Jogging is allowed only when the child is at least **8 months old**.

Do not jog with a child younger than **8 months old**.

For children under **8 months old**, use the TrailPro Jogger only at a normal walking pace.

Before jogging, confirm that the child has sufficient head and neck control and is properly secured in the five-point harness.

---

## 4. Approved Use

The TrailPro Jogger Stroller is approved for:

- Walking on smooth sidewalks
- Walking on paved park paths
- Jogging on smooth paved paths
- Jogging on flat running paths
- Everyday outdoor use on approved surfaces

The stroller may be used for jogging only when the front wheel is locked and the child meets the jogging age requirement.

---

## 5. Prohibited Use

Do not use the TrailPro Jogger Stroller for:

- Jogging with a child younger than 8 months old
- Jogging when the front wheel is unlocked
- Running at high speed
- Skating
- Biking
- Going up or down stairs with the child seated
- Riding on escalators
- Carrying more than one child
- Transporting pets
- Carrying heavy bags on the handlebar
- Rough off-road trails
- Sand, deep gravel, mud, snow, or rocky terrain

Do not use the stroller on stairs or escalators.

---

## 6. Jogging Safety Requirements

Before jogging, confirm all of the following:

- The child is at least **8 months old**.
- The child weighs no more than **55 lb**.
- The five-point harness is securely fastened.
- The front wheel is locked in the forward position.
- The parking brake is fully released.
- The tires are properly attached.
- The path is smooth, paved, and free of major obstacles.
- The wrist strap is attached to the adult user.

Do not jog if any safety requirement is not met.

---

## 7. Front Wheel Lock

The TrailPro Jogger includes a lockable front wheel.

Use the swivel mode for walking and slow turns.

Use the locked forward position for jogging.

To lock the front wheel:

1. Stop the stroller completely.
2. Position the front wheel so it points straight forward.
3. Move the front wheel lock lever into the locked position.
4. Gently push the stroller forward to confirm the wheel stays straight.

To unlock the front wheel:

1. Stop the stroller completely.
2. Move the front wheel lock lever into the unlocked position.
3. Confirm the wheel swivels freely before walking.

The front wheel must be locked before jogging.

---

## 8. Wrist Strap

The TrailPro Jogger includes a wrist safety strap.

When jogging or walking downhill:

1. Place the strap around your wrist.
2. Hold the handlebar with both hands.
3. Keep the strap attached until the stroller is stopped and the parking brake is engaged.

Always use the wrist strap when jogging.

---

## 9. Safety Warnings

Failure to follow these instructions may result in injury.

Always secure the child with the five-point harness.

Never leave the child unattended in the stroller.

Always engage the parking brake before placing the child into the stroller or removing the child from the stroller.

Do not hang bags, purses, backpacks, or shopping items from the handlebar. Hanging weight from the handlebar may cause the stroller to tip backward.

Do not allow the child to stand in the stroller seat.

Do not allow the child to climb into or out of the stroller without adult assistance.

Do not use the stroller if any part is cracked, torn, loose, missing, or not working correctly.

---

## 10. Harness Instructions

The TrailPro Jogger includes a five-point harness.

To secure the child:

1. Place the child fully back in the seat.
2. Position the shoulder straps over the child’s shoulders.
3. Position the waist straps around the child’s waist.
4. Insert both buckle tongues into the center buckle until each side clicks.
5. Pull the adjustment straps until the harness is snug.

The harness should be snug but not too tight. You should be able to fit one finger between the harness and the child.

Always check that the buckle is fully locked before walking or jogging.

---

## 11. Brake Instructions

The TrailPro Jogger includes a rear parking brake.

To engage the brake:

1. Stop the stroller completely.
2. Press the brake pedal down with your foot.
3. Gently push the stroller forward and backward to confirm the wheels do not move.

To release the brake:

1. Place one hand on the handlebar.
2. Lift the brake pedal upward with your foot.
3. Confirm that both rear wheels move freely.

Always engage the brake before placing the child into the stroller or removing the child from the stroller.

Do not jog while the parking brake is engaged.

---

## 12. If the Brake Does Not Lock

If the brake does not lock:

1. Stop using the stroller immediately.
2. Remove the child from the stroller.
3. Check for debris around the rear wheels and brake pedal.
4. Clear any visible dirt, leaves, small stones, or string.
5. Try engaging the brake again without the child seated.
6. If the brake still does not lock, discontinue use and contact customer support.

Do not use the stroller if the brake cannot securely stop both rear wheels.

---

## 13. Folding Instructions

Before folding the stroller:

- Remove the child.
- Empty the storage basket.
- Engage the brake.
- Unlock the front wheel.
- Fold the canopy back.
- Make sure no objects are blocking the frame.

To fold the stroller:

1. Stand behind the stroller.
2. Press the frame release button on the handlebar.
3. Pull the folding strap upward from the center of the seat.
4. Guide the handlebar forward until the frame folds.
5. Confirm that the side latch clicks into the locked folded position.

Do not force the stroller to fold. If the frame does not fold smoothly, check for objects blocking the folding joints.

---

## 14. Unfolding Instructions

To unfold the stroller:

1. Place the folded stroller on a flat surface.
2. Release the side latch.
3. Hold the handlebar and lift upward.
4. Continue lifting until the frame clicks into the open position.
5. Press down gently on the seat area to confirm the frame is locked.

Do not place the child in the stroller until the frame is fully locked open.

---

## 15. Storage Basket

The storage basket is located below the seat.

Maximum basket weight: **12 lb**

Do not place sharp, fragile, hot, or loose items in the storage basket.

Do not allow the child to sit, stand, or climb into the storage basket.

Do not exceed the **12 lb** storage basket limit.

---

## 16. Canopy Use

The canopy may be extended to provide shade.

To use the canopy:

1. Pull the front edge of the canopy forward.
2. Adjust the canopy until it covers the desired area.
3. Push the canopy backward when not in use.

The canopy does not provide full protection from sun, heat, wind, rain, or cold.

Do not use the canopy as a carrying handle.

---

## 17. Cleaning Instructions

Clean the stroller regularly, especially after outdoor use.

Frame cleaning:

- Wipe the frame with a damp cloth.
- Use mild soap if needed.
- Dry the frame with a clean towel.
- Do not use bleach, abrasive cleaners, or harsh solvents.

Fabric cleaning:

- Spot clean only.
- Use cold water and mild soap.
- Air dry completely before use.
- Do not machine wash.
- Do not tumble dry.
- Do not iron.

Wheel cleaning:

- Remove visible dirt from the wheels.
- Wipe wheels with a damp cloth.
- Check for stones, hair, string, or debris around the axles.
- Allow wheels to dry before storage.

---

## 18. Tire and Wheel Care

Before each use, check that:

- All wheels are securely attached.
- The front wheel locks correctly.
- The front wheel swivels freely when unlocked.
- The rear wheels roll smoothly.
- No debris is wrapped around the axles.

If the wheels make noise or do not turn smoothly:

1. Check for debris near each wheel axle.
2. Remove visible dirt, string, or hair.
3. Wipe the wheels with a damp cloth.
4. Let the wheels dry.
5. If the issue continues, stop using the stroller and contact customer support.

Do not apply household oil or grease to the wheels unless instructed by customer support.

---

## 19. Maintenance

Before each use, check that:

- The frame locks open securely.
- The brake stops both rear wheels.
- The front wheel locks in the forward position.
- The wheels roll smoothly.
- The harness buckles lock correctly.
- The seat fabric is not torn.
- No screws, rivets, or frame parts are loose.
- The folding latch works correctly.
- The wrist strap is attached and not damaged.

Do not use the stroller if it appears damaged or unsafe.

---

## 20. Car Seat Compatibility

The TrailPro Jogger is not compatible with infant car seats unless a TrailPro-approved car seat adapter is installed.

Do not attach a car seat, bassinet, board, second seat, or third-party accessory unless the accessory is specifically approved by TrailPro.

Do not jog while using a car seat adapter.

Using unapproved accessories may make the stroller unstable.

---

## 21. Weather Guidance

The TrailPro Jogger may be used in light weather conditions, but it is not designed for extreme weather.

Do not use the stroller during:

- Heavy rain
- Snow
- Ice
- Strong wind
- Extreme heat
- Extreme cold

Do not jog on wet, icy, sandy, muddy, or uneven surfaces.

If the stroller becomes wet, wipe the frame and wheels dry before storage.

Allow fabric to air dry completely before folding or storing.

---

## 22. Transportation and Storage

When transporting the stroller:

- Fold the stroller completely.
- Confirm the side latch is locked.
- Lift the stroller by the frame.
- Do not lift the stroller by the canopy, tray, harness, wheels, or wrist strap.

When storing the stroller:

- Store indoors when possible.
- Keep the stroller dry.
- Avoid direct sunlight for long periods.
- Keep away from heaters or open flames.
- Do not stack heavy objects on top of the stroller.

---

## 23. When to Stop Using the Stroller

Stop using the TrailPro Jogger immediately if:

- The child weighs more than 55 lb.
- The child is younger than 8 months old and the stroller is being used for jogging.
- The front wheel does not lock before jogging.
- The brake does not lock.
- The frame does not lock open.
- The harness buckle does not close securely.
- A wheel is loose or damaged.
- The wrist strap is torn or missing.
- The stroller tips, bends, cracks, or becomes unstable.
- Any part is missing or broken.

Contact customer support before using the stroller again.
