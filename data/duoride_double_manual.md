# DuoRide Double Stroller User Manual

**Model:** DuoRide DR-300  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Manual Type:** Synthetic portfolio manual for RAG evaluation project

---

## 1. Product Overview

The DuoRide Double Stroller is a two-seat stroller designed for walking, errands, parks, paved paths, and everyday family use.

The DuoRide Double is intended for up to two children, one child per seat.

The DuoRide Double is not designed for jogging, running, skating, biking, stair use, escalator use, or rough off-road terrain.

---

## 2. Child Weight and Seat Limits

Each seat of the DuoRide Double Stroller has a maximum child weight limit of **40 lb per seat**.

The stroller may be used only when each child meets all of the following requirements:

- The child can sit upright without assistance.
- The child weighs no more than **40 lb** in their assigned seat.
- The child is secured using the built-in five-point harness.
- The child fits comfortably in the seat without leaning outside the stroller frame.

Do not place a child weighing more than **40 lb** in either seat.

Do not place more than one child in a single seat.

Do not exceed two children total.

---

## 3. Approved Use

The DuoRide Double Stroller is approved for:

- Walking on smooth sidewalks
- Indoor shopping areas
- Parks with paved paths
- Public transportation platforms
- Everyday city use
- Walking with one or two children seated properly

The stroller should only be used at a normal walking pace.

---

## 4. Prohibited Use

Do not use the DuoRide Double Stroller for:

- Jogging
- Running
- Skating
- Biking
- Going up or down stairs with children seated
- Riding on escalators
- Carrying more than two children
- Placing more than one child in a single seat
- Transporting pets
- Carrying heavy bags on the handlebar
- Off-road trails, sand, deep gravel, mud, snow, or rocky terrain

The DuoRide Double Stroller is **not approved for jogging or running**.

---

## 5. Safety Warnings

Failure to follow these instructions may result in injury.

Always secure each child with the five-point harness for their seat.

Never leave children unattended in the stroller.

Always engage the parking brake before placing children into the stroller or removing children from the stroller.

### Handlebar Bags and Hanging Items

Do not hang bags, purses, backpacks, or shopping items from the handlebar. Hanging weight from the handlebar may cause the stroller to tip backward.

Do not allow children to stand in the stroller seats.

Do not allow children to climb into or out of the stroller without adult assistance.

Do not use the stroller if any part is cracked, torn, loose, missing, or not working correctly.

---

## 6. Two-Seat Safety Rules

Each seat must be used according to its own weight and harness limits.

When using the stroller with two children:

- Place one child in each seat.
- Secure each child with the harness before moving the stroller.
- Confirm both children are seated upright.
- Confirm neither child is leaning outside the stroller frame.
- Do not allow children to swap seats while the stroller is moving.
- Do not place a child in the storage basket.

The stroller may be used with only one child, but the child must still be seated in one of the stroller seats and secured with the harness.

---

## 7. Harness Instructions

The DuoRide Double Stroller includes one five-point harness for each seat.

To secure each child:

1. Place the child fully back in the seat.
2. Position the shoulder straps over the child’s shoulders.
3. Position the waist straps around the child’s waist.
4. Insert the buckle tongues into the center buckle until each side clicks.
5. Pull the adjustment straps until the harness is snug.

The harness should be snug but not too tight. You should be able to fit one finger between the harness and the child.

Always check that both harnesses are fully locked before using the stroller.

---

## 8. Brake Instructions

The DuoRide Double Stroller includes a rear parking brake.

To engage the brake:

1. Stop the stroller completely.
2. Press the brake pedal down with your foot.
3. Gently push the stroller forward and backward to confirm the wheels do not move.

To release the brake:

1. Place one hand on the handlebar.
2. Lift the brake pedal upward with your foot.
3. Confirm that both rear wheels move freely.

Always engage the brake before placing children into the stroller or removing children from the stroller.

---

## 9. If the Brake Does Not Lock

If the brake does not lock:

1. Stop using the stroller immediately.
2. Remove all children from the stroller.
3. Check for debris around the rear wheels and brake pedal.
4. Clear any visible dirt, leaves, small stones, or string.
5. Try engaging the brake again without children seated.
6. If the brake still does not lock, discontinue use and contact customer support.

Do not use the stroller if the brake cannot securely stop both rear wheels.

---

## 10. Folding Instructions

Before folding the stroller:

- Remove all children.
- Empty the storage basket.
- Engage the brake.
- Fold both canopies back.
- Make sure no objects are blocking the frame.
- Confirm both seats are empty.

To fold the stroller:

1. Stand behind the stroller.
2. Press both frame release buttons on the handlebar.
3. Pull the folding strap upward from the center of the stroller frame.
4. Guide the handlebar forward until the frame folds.
5. Confirm that the side latch clicks into the locked folded position.

Do not force the stroller to fold. If the frame does not fold smoothly, check for objects blocking the folding joints or seat areas.

---

## 11. Unfolding Instructions

To unfold the stroller:

1. Place the folded stroller on a flat surface.
2. Release the side latch.
3. Hold the handlebar and lift upward.
4. Continue lifting until the frame clicks into the open position.
5. Press down gently on both seat areas to confirm the frame is locked.

Do not place children in the stroller until the frame is fully locked open.

---

## 12. Storage Basket

The storage basket is located below the seats.

Maximum basket weight: **15 lb**

Do not place sharp, fragile, hot, or loose items in the storage basket.

Do not allow a child to sit, stand, or climb into the storage basket.

Do not exceed the **15 lb** storage basket limit.

---

## 13. Canopy Use

The DuoRide Double Stroller includes two adjustable canopies, one for each seat.

To use a canopy:

1. Pull the front edge of the canopy forward.
2. Adjust the canopy until it covers the desired area.
3. Push the canopy backward when not in use.

The canopies do not provide full protection from sun, heat, wind, rain, or cold.

Do not use either canopy as a carrying handle.

---

## 14. Cleaning Instructions

Clean the stroller regularly.

Frame cleaning:

- Wipe the frame with a damp cloth.
- Use mild soap if needed.
- Dry the frame with a clean towel.
- Do not use bleach, abrasive cleaners, or harsh solvents.

Fabric cleaning:

- Spot clean only.
- Use cold water and mild soap.
- Air dry completely before use.
- Do not machine wash.
- Do not tumble dry.
- Do not iron.

Wheel cleaning:

- Remove visible dirt from the wheels.
- Wipe wheels with a damp cloth.
- Check for stones, hair, string, or debris around the axles.
- Allow wheels to dry before storage.

---

## 15. Maintenance

Before each use, check that:

- The frame locks open securely.
- The brake stops both rear wheels.
- The wheels roll smoothly.
- Both harness buckles lock correctly.
- Both seat fabrics are not torn.
- No screws, rivets, or frame parts are loose.
- The folding latch works correctly.
- Both canopies are securely attached.

Do not use the stroller if it appears damaged or unsafe.

---

## 16. Wheel Care

The DuoRide Double Stroller is designed for smooth surfaces.

Avoid using the stroller on:

- Sand
- Deep gravel
- Mud
- Snow
- Rocky paths
- Uneven trails

If the wheels make noise or do not turn smoothly:

1. Check for debris near the wheel axles.
2. Remove visible dirt, string, or hair.
3. Wipe the wheels with a damp cloth.
4. Let the wheels dry.
5. If the issue continues, stop using the stroller and contact customer support.

Do not apply household oil or grease to the wheels unless instructed by customer support.

---

## 17. Car Seat Compatibility

The DuoRide Double Stroller is not compatible with infant car seats unless a DuoRide-approved adapter is installed.

Only one approved car seat adapter may be used at a time.

Do not attach two infant car seats unless the accessory is specifically approved by DuoRide.

Do not attach a standing board, third seat, bassinet, or third-party accessory unless it is specifically approved by DuoRide.

Using unapproved accessories may make the stroller unstable.

---

## 18. Weather Guidance

The DuoRide Double Stroller may be used in light weather conditions, but it is not designed for extreme weather.

Do not use the stroller during:

- Heavy rain
- Snow
- Ice
- Strong wind
- Extreme heat
- Extreme cold

If the stroller becomes wet, wipe the frame and wheels dry before storage.

Allow fabric to air dry completely before folding or storing.

---

## 19. Transportation and Storage

When transporting the stroller:

- Fold the stroller completely.
- Confirm the side latch is locked.
- Lift the stroller by the frame.
- Do not lift the stroller by the canopy, tray, harness, wheels, or storage basket.

When storing the stroller:

- Store indoors when possible.
- Keep the stroller dry.
- Avoid direct sunlight for long periods.
- Keep away from heaters or open flames.
- Do not stack heavy objects on top of the stroller.

---

## 20. When to Stop Using the Stroller

Stop using the DuoRide Double Stroller immediately if:

- Either child weighs more than **40 lb**.
- More than two children are being placed in the stroller.
- A child is placed in the storage basket.
- The brake does not lock.
- The frame does not lock open.
- Either harness buckle does not close securely.
- A wheel is loose or damaged.
- The stroller tips, bends, cracks, or becomes unstable.
- Any part is missing or broken.

Contact customer support before using the stroller again.
